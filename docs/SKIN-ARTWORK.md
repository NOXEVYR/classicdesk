# 0.10 新皮肤插画

三款背景通过内置 image_gen 工具独立生成，无参考图，未使用 CLI/API 回退。新增插画均为无人物场景，用于软件界面及预览，不会设置系统壁纸。

## 樱月

[原始 PNG](../src/ClassicDesk/Assets/skin-sakura.png)

### 完整提示词

One panoramic wallpaper illustration for a refined desktop app skin. Original Japanese anime background art, NO people, NO animals, NO characters, NO text, NO interface. Very wide landscape 3:1 composition. Dreamy pale sakura pink and lavender twilight, a small luminous crescent moon far in the upper right, a few softly painted cherry blossom branches entering only from the far right edge, soft misty violet distant mountain silhouettes and pearl-white clouds. Almost white quiet open sky through the left two thirds for readable dark interface text. Carefully composed, delicate clean painted edges, elegant soft cel shading, gentle cinematic atmosphere, not photorealistic, no noisy particles. Avoid all green and teal. Designed as a subtle premium anime wallpaper, pastel airy and beautiful.
